#Fututre Requirement(FR)

FR-001

Atlas shall provide a visual representation of all major system components.

FR-002

Users shall be able to inspect any component.

FR-003

Users shall be able to view dependencies between modules.

FR-004

Atlas shall explain system concepts using integrated documentation.

FR-005

Atlas shall support AI-assisted exploration.

FR-006

Atlas shall provide a sandbox for experimentation.

FR-007

Atlas shall allow rollback of experimental changes.
